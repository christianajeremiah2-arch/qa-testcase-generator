User Story: As a registered user, I want to log in with my email and password so that I can access my dashboard.

Acceptance Criteria:
1. The email field must contain a valid email format.
2. The password must be between 8 and 20 characters.
3. After 3 consecutive failed attempts, the account is locked for 15 minutes.
4. On successful login, the user is redirected to the dashboard.
5. Error messages must not reveal whether the email or the password was wrong.
