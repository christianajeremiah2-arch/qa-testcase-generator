Requirement: Birth Registration form (Registrar portal)

1. The Registrar can save a birth registration as a draft or submit it for approval.
2. Child's first name and surname are required and accept letters, hyphens and apostrophes only.
3. Date of birth cannot be in the future.
4. Gender must be selected from Male or Female.
5. A submitted record shows status "Pending" until an Approving Officer approves or queries it.
6. A queried record returns to the Registrar with the query reason visible.
