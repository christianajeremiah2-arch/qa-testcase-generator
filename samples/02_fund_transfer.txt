User Story: As a customer, I want to transfer money to another bank account so that I can pay people.

Acceptance Criteria:
1. The beneficiary account number must be exactly 10 digits (NUBAN).
2. The minimum transfer amount is NGN 100 and the maximum per transaction is NGN 1,000,000.
3. The customer must enter a 4-digit transaction PIN to confirm.
4. The transfer is rejected if the amount is greater than the available balance.
5. A successful transfer shows a receipt with a transaction reference.
